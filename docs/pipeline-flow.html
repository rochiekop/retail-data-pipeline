Daily Orders Pipeline

How one Business Date moves from the Olist shop into a ClickHouse star schema. Postgres and Excel are the sources. Python extracts and loads them unchanged into Bronze, and dbt transforms Bronze into Silver and Gold inside the warehouse. Airflow runs the four steps in order. Kafka is designed in but arrives in a later slice.

Architecture

built in slice 1 later slice Rerunning a Business Date drops its partitions and rebuilds them, so a rerun changes nothing.
